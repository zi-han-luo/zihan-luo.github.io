About Me

I am a master's student at the University of Chinese Academy of Sciences, advised by Prof. Jialin Zhang and Prof. Xiaoming Sun at the Institute of Computing Technology, Chinese Academy of Sciences.

I will join the Department of Industrial Engineering and Decision Analytics at The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology as a Ph.D. student, under the supervision of Prof. Yiding Feng.

My research interests include approximation algorithms and mechanism design, with a focus on facility location games, fair division, and auction theory.
